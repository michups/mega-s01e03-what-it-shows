Context

The review of incident 4471 demonstrates different causes under one ticket. Matching subjects alone can merge unrelated defects, while different subjects can describe one defect. The old decision remains unchanged as the record of what the desk accepted in 2024.

Proposed decision

Replace subject-only deduplication with demonstrated cause: identify the tenant configuration, locale, form version, entry channel, and actual symptom before merging. A shared subject is a search aid, not causal evidence. Missing context stays unplaced until investigated.
